    Data dictionary

    What each column of the collection data holds, and how the three files fit together.

    The downloads hold three tables, each as CSV and as JSON: species, held lines and media recipes. A held line is a cell line the lab still keeps, live, in cryo, or with neither yet recorded. The README in the zip describes the same columns, in the same words.

    How the files fit together

    • A held line's species_id names its species in the species file.
    • The Media Codes in a held line's media are the medium of recipes in the media file. A medium may have no recipe there yet.
    • Each line and species has a page of its own: line/ and its Culture ID (spaces as hyphens), and species/ and its species_id.

    Species

    pccl-species-2026-11-12.csv and pccl-species-2026-11-12.json. One row per species with at least one held line.

    species_id
    PCCL's ID for the species, as in its page address. Never given to another species.
    name
    The species' accepted scientific name, as the lab uses it.
    authorship
    The name's author citation, where the name has been matched to World Flora Online. Empty otherwise.
    genus
    Its genus.
    family
    Its family.
    order
    Its order.
    common_names
    Its common names from the lab's records, separated by "; ".
    species_activities
    Bioactivities reported for the species, separated by "; ".
    genus_activities
    Bioactivities reported for its genus, separated by "; ".
    wfo_id
    Its World Flora Online ID, where the name has been matched. Empty otherwise.
    ipni_id
    Its International Plant Names Index (IPNI) ID, where the name has been matched. Empty otherwise.
    held_lines
    How many held lines of the species the collection has.

    Held lines

    pccl-lines-2026-11-12.csv and pccl-lines-2026-11-12.json. One row per held line: a cell line the lab still keeps, live, in cryo, or with neither yet recorded. A line can be kept as more than one stock, on different media: the CSV gives the line one row that combines its stocks, and the JSON lists each stock with its own medium, status and growth.

    line_id
    PCCL's ID for the line. Never given to another line.
    culture_id
    The lab's Culture ID. The line's page address is the Culture ID with spaces as hyphens.
    species_id
    The species' ID, as in the species file.
    species
    The species' accepted name.
    status
    Live & cryopreserved, Cryopreserved, or Not recorded: live if any of its stocks is kept live, cryopreserved if any is cryopreserved.
    media
    The Media Codes of the media its stocks are kept on, separated by "; ". Empty where none is recorded. A medium may have no recipe in the media file yet.
    growth
    The highest growth rate recorded for its stocks, from 1 (slow) to 5 (fast). Empty where none is recorded.
    available_to_request
    true if the line can be requested.
    stocks
    In the JSON only: each stock the line is kept as, with its own medium (a Media Code, or null where none is recorded), status and growth, where the CSV combines them in one row.

    Media recipes

    pccl-media-2026-11-12.csv and pccl-media-2026-11-12.json. One row per reagent of each recipe, in the order the medium is made. Amounts are per liter of medium.

    medium
    The Media Code, as in the lines file and the medium's page address.
    ph
    The pH the medium is adjusted to.
    step
    When the reagent goes in. components: dissolved first, before the volume is made up to 1 L and the pH adjusted. gel: the gelling agent, added before autoclaving. vitamins: added after autoclaving, before pouring.
    reagent
    The reagent.
    stock
    The concentration of the stock solution it is added from, where it is added from one.
    amount
    How much to add per liter of medium, in the unit given.
    unit
    The unit of the amount.
    vendor
    The supplier the lab buys it from.
    catalog_number
    The supplier's catalog number.
    steps
    In the JSON only: the recipe's reagents under the step each goes in (components, gel, vitamins), each with its reagent, stock, amount, unit, vendor and catalog_number, as in the CSV.

    B122

    Growth medium · 51 cell lines grown in it

    At a glance

    Base
    Gamborg B5 salts
    pH
    5.8
    Gelling agent
    Gelrite 5 g
    Autoclave
    121 °C, 15 to 20 min
    Vitamins
    After autoclaving

    Lab recipe

    For 1 liter, in the order it is made

    Last changed on or before 3 October 2024, while the collection was at UMass Amherst. Not yet reviewed at Tufts.

    1. 1 Dissolve the components

      B5 Salts & Organics3.1 gPhytotechlab G768
      2,4-Dichlorophenoxyacetic acid (2,4-D)0.1 mL of a 10 mg/mL stockAlfa Aesar A12467
      Kinetin0.1 mL of a 1 mg/mL stockAlfa Aesar 177254
      Coconut Water100 mLVita Coco
      Glutamine1.46 gAlfa Aesar A14201
      Banana extract100 mL of a 100 mg/mL stockPhytotechlab B852
      Sucrose30 gFisher S5*12

    2. 2 Make up to 1 L with water and adjust the pH to 5.8

    3. 3 Add the gelling agent

      Gelrite5 gPlantmedia.com 30634003-4

    4. 4 Autoclave at 121 °C for 15 to 20 minutes

    5. 5 Add the vitamin solution, then pour

      Gamborg B5 Vitamin Mix, 112 mg/mL (1,000X)1 mLPlantmedia.com 30630027-1

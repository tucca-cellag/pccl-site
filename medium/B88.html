    B88

    A growth medium built on Gamborg B5 salts, at pH 5.8. The library holds 86 lines grown in it, all kept cryopreserved.

    Request a line

    Cite this medium

    https://pccl.tufts.edu/medium/B88

    Base
    Gamborg B5 salts
    pH
    5.8
    Gelling agent
    Gelrite 5 g
    Autoclave
    121 °C, 15 to 20 min
    Vitamins
    After autoclaving

    Lab recipe

    For 1 liter, in the order it is made.

    1. Dissolve the components

      B5 Salts & Organics3.1 gPhytotechlab G768
      2,4-Dichlorophenoxyacetic acid (2,4-D)0.1 mL of a 10 mg/mL stockAlfa Aesar A12467
      Kinetin0.1 mL of a 1 mg/mL stockAlfa Aesar 177254
      Coconut Water100 mLVita Coco
      Sucrose30 gFisher S5*12

    2. Make up to 1 L with water and adjust the pH to 5.8

    3. Add the gelling agent

      Gelrite5 gPlantmedia.com 30634003-4

    4. Autoclave at 121 °C for 15 to 20 minutes

    5. Add the vitamin solution, then pour

      Gamborg B5 Vitamin Mix, 112 mg/mL (1,000X)1 mLPlantmedia.com 30630027-1
